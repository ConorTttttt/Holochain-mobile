## Scenarios

| Scenario | Result | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| invalid note is rejected on the phone | pass | rejected by the phone's own validation before publishing |
| receipts wait in the linker while the phone is killed | pass | authorities fetched the note from the linker with the phone killed; their receipts waited in the linker's inbox and were delivered after restart (authority read with phone killed 14.8 ms, first receipt held after the kill 6.78 s, linker acceptance after create returned 37.7 ms, phone offline for 6.80 s, receipts complete after restart 103.8 ms) |
| linker crash and restart | pass | 207 acceptances survived the crash; the note written during the outage was published after reconnecting (outage note visible after restart 56.66 s, phone reconnected after restart 315.1 ms) |
