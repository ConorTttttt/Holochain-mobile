## Scenarios

| Scenario | Result | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| invalid note is rejected on the phone | pass | rejected by the phone's own validation before publishing |
| receipts wait in the linker while the phone is killed | pass | authorities fetched the note from the linker with the phone killed; their receipts waited in the linker's inbox and were delivered after restart (authority read with phone killed 2.3 ms, first receipt held after the kill 309.0 ms, linker acceptance after create returned 51.9 ms, phone offline for 311.3 ms, receipts complete after restart 102.8 ms) |
| linker crash and restart | pass | 12 acceptances survived the crash; the note written during the outage was published after reconnecting (outage note visible after restart 58.73 s, phone reconnected after restart 306.7 ms) |
