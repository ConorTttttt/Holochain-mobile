## Scenarios

| Scenario | Result | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| invalid note is rejected on the phone | pass | rejected by the phone's own validation before publishing |
| receipts wait in the linker while the phone is killed | FAIL | receipts arrived before the kill, so the inbox was not exercised |
| linker crash and restart | pass | 212 acceptances survived the crash; the note written during the outage was published after reconnecting (outage note visible after restart 59.74 s, phone reconnected after restart 205.0 ms) |
